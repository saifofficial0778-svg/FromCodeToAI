# Notes for: Search the height in metres of the Eiffel Tower and of the Burj Khalifa. Then calculate (1) what percentage of the Burj Khalifa's height the Eiffel Tower is, and (2) how many Eiffel Towers stacked on top of each other would reach the Burj Khalifa's height.

## Round 1 — ask_search_llm
**Task:** Find the exact height in metres of the Eiffel Tower in Paris and the height in metres of the Burj Khalifa in Dubai. Provide the numbers and the source URLs for each.

**Answer:**
- Eiffel Tower (Paris): **330 m** – https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eiffel_Tower  
- Burj Khalifa (Dubai): **829.8 m** – https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burj_Khalifa

## Round 2 — ask_maths_llm
**Task:** Using the heights: Eiffel Tower = 330 meters, Burj Khalifa = 829.8 meters. Calculate (1) the percentage that the Eiffel Tower's height represents of the Burj Khalifa's height, expressed to two decimal places, and (2) how many Eiffel Towers stacked would reach the Burj Khalifa's height, expressed to two decimal places.

**Answer:**
**Step 1 – Percentage of Eiffel Tower height relative to Burj Khalifa**

\[
\frac{330}{829.8}\times 100 = 39.76861894432393\%
\]

Rounded to two decimal places: **39.77 %**

**Step 2 – Number of Eiffel Towers needed to equal Burj Khalifa height**

\[
\frac{829.8}{330} = 2.514545454545454
\]

Rounded to two decimal places: **2.51** Eiffel Towers

**Answers**

1. 39.77 %  
2. 2.51 Eiffel Towers (stacked)
